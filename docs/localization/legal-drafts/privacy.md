## English draft — Pending Legal Approval

```html
<p>This Policy explains what data the Bremo application collects, who can see it, and how long it is retained.</p>
<h2>Data we collect</h2>
<ul>
<li><strong>Account:</strong> name, email address, phone number, and profile photograph, if provided. The password is stored in encrypted form and cannot be read.</li>
<li><strong>Addresses:</strong> the full address and location point on the map, for carrying out the service.</li>
<li><strong>Request:</strong> the service, problem type, description, and appointment time, together with attached photographs, video, and audio recordings. Location metadata embedded in photographs is removed.</li>
<li><strong>Conversations:</strong> the text of messages and photographs sent through the application.</li>
<li><strong>Payment:</strong> amounts, payment method, and transaction references only. For InstaPay transfers, the transfer reference number and a photograph of the receipt, if attached. Bank card details are not stored.</li>
<li><strong>Ratings</strong> submitted by both parties.</li>
<li><strong>For technicians:</strong> identity documents and photographs, stored privately for management only; payment details for receiving amounts due, stored in encrypted form; and the technician’s location only while travelling to the customer.</li>
</ul>
<h2>Location</h2>
<ul>
<li>The technician’s location is sent approximately every 30 seconds only while the status is “On the way”. Only the customer who owns the request can see it.</li>
<li>Route points are deleted when the request is closed or cancelled. The arrival location is retained for 90 days to resolve any dispute.</li>
</ul>
<h2>Who can see the data</h2>
<ul>
<li><strong>Before the request is confirmed,</strong> the technician sees only the area name, without the full address or phone number.</li>
<li><strong>After confirmation,</strong> the assigned technician sees the full address. Each party’s phone number is shown to the other until 24 hours after the request is closed.</li>
<li>In chat before confirmation, numbers, links, and email addresses are automatically masked. The original text is stored in encrypted form and management accesses it only in the event of a problem or report.</li>
<li><strong>The visit-sharing link</strong> shows only the technician’s first name and the request status, without an address, map, phone number, or price. The link expires when the request ends.</li>
<li><strong>The management team</strong> sees the information needed for operations and resolving problems. Financial actions are restricted to the general administrator.</li>
</ul>
<h2>Retention periods</h2>
<ul>
<li>Temporary media not attached to a request: 24 hours.</li>
<li>Arrival location: 90 days.</li>
<li>Identity documents of a rejected technician: 90 days after final rejection, after which they are deleted.</li>
<li>Requests, events, and payments: retained permanently and anonymised when the account is deleted.</li>
</ul>
<h2>Account deletion</h2>
<p>An account can be deleted from “My account” in the application, provided there is no active request. Personal account information is removed upon deletion, while request and payment records are retained in anonymised form.</p>
<h2>Email and notifications</h2>
<p>Email is used for verification, password recovery, and important request notifications. In-app notifications are used to follow the request.</p>
<h2>Contact</h2>
<p>For any query about data, use “Help and support” or the support address at the bottom of this page.</p>
```
